RESEARCH GUIDE: does a ticker fit the bracket strategy?
=======================================================
The live monitor (t_order_manager.py) rests a SELL at MA(6) + X% and a BUY at
MA(6) - X% on 5M candles. A ticker "fits" only if, at some band width X:
  (a) price reaches the band on both sides often enough to actually trade;
  (b) each reach is a real multi-5M-candle sweep with elevated volume, not a
      single low-volume flash (single-tick noise can't be trusted as a fill);
  (c) price keeps running PAST the level once reached (headroom for widening);
  (d) the swing clears costs: broker fee ~0.05%/leg (~0.1% round-trip), so
      prefer swings >= ~0.3-0.5%, i.e. X in the 0.2-0.5% zone;
  (e) the deep dips that would fill the BUY are not only regime-break crashes
      (live buying is disabled in a regime break, so those events are wasted).

Run every acceptance step with --interval 5M: that is the bar the strategy
brackets actually rest against. Commands below use SAFE as the example; swap in
the candidate ticker. A full-year 5M run takes ~40s (sandbox is server-bound);
prefer --from/--to windows for quick iterations.

Read a row as: "with a band of X%, the bracket fires N 5M bars over D days/year
and the farthest single candle strayed M% past the MA". SAFE's tradable zone is
the 0.3-0.5% band: tens of fires/year on each side, each a real sweep. The huge
maxima (+23%/-37%) are single blow-off/crash days, NOT typical tradable events.

REFERENCE EVENT (peak sweep, 2025-10-20): resting SELL at 16.60 would have
filled on 4 consecutive 5M candles; the market ran +12.0% avg / +20.5% max PAST
the level before reverting. That past-the-level run is the reason a wider band
(or asymmetric sell_band_factor) collects more.

RESEARCH RUNBOOK FOR A CANDIDATE TICKER
---------------------------------------
Phase A - coarse gate (fast: D + 30M). Stop early if the ticker has no structure.
  1. history_candles_peak_inspect.py --tickers T --interval D --top 15
     -> biggest daily moves. If the largest outliers are < ~0.5%, the ticker is
        too flat for a 5M MA-bracket strategy -> reject.
  2. history_candles_peak_inspect.py --tickers T --interval 30M --profile --bands "0.15 0.5 1 2"
     -> both sides? If UP or DOWN shows ~0 pokes past 0.5% on 30M, reject.

Phase B - acceptance on the strategy time frame (full-year 5M, ~40s).
  3. history_candles_peak_inspect.py --tickers T --interval 5M --profile
     -> compare both tables to the SAFE baseline above. Requirement: BOTH sides
        have real activity at 0.3-0.5% (SAFE: ~37/16 up, ~50/12 down at 0.3%;
        ~23/7 up, ~30/7 down at 0.5%). One-sided tickers fit half the strategy.
     -> pick X so each side fires roughly 10-60 days/year. For SAFE X ~ 0.3-0.5%.
  4. Verify the chosen band on the actual spike days found in step 1:
     history_candles_peak_inspect.py --tickers T --interval 5M --side sell --band X --from <d0> --to <d1>
     ... and the same with --side buy for a crash day.
     -> confirms the bracket would have fired, and records how far price ran
        past X (avg/median/max). Use that to set deviation_percent and the
        asymmetric sell_band_factor / buy_band_factor in ticker_settings.json.

Phase C - noise vs sweep. The tool counts candles/bars, which cannot tell a
          real sweep from a one-candle wick. Verify by zooming with --raw (see
          "USING --raw (ZOOM) PROPERLY" below) on the events you intend to trust.
  5. Take the exact fire rows from the step-4 band test (they carry precise
     datetime + volume) and zoom the strongest one on each side:
     history_candles_peak_inspect.py --tickers T --interval 1M --raw \
         --from "<day> <HH:MM>" --to "<day> <HH:MM>"
  6. Repeat for the top 3-5 UP events AND top 3-5 DOWN events. If most "fires"
     on either side are 1-candle wicks or open-gap days, that side is noise ->
     don't widen the band into it, or reject the ticker.

USING --raw (ZOOM) PROPERLY
---------------------------
WHEN to zoom:
  * Phase C of every candidate, on both sides. Fires/yr counts alone are not
    enough: they can look fine while the real character is wicks (see below).
  * Whenever a D-scan day ranks as a big "peak". A daily deviation is measured
    against that day's own SMA, so a day that gap-opens at its high (then
    drifts) ranks top even though nothing moves intraday - and a resting
    bracket measured off the intraday MA(6) has nothing real to fill there.
  * To zoom the EXACT fire, take its time from the band-test matched-candle
    table instead of guessing from the daily candle.
HOW to zoom:
  * Use 1M and a tight time window so the output stays readable:
    history_candles_peak_inspect.py --tickers T --interval 1M --raw \
        --from "<day> <HH:MM>" --to "<day> <HH:MM>"
    (time-of-day in --from/--to is supported; --interval 5M for a wide view.)
  * Compare the move-minutes' volume with the ticker's own typical 1M volume.
    A real event shows volume an order of magnitude or more above median.
  * Count the consecutive candles that push price through your level.
READ the result - REAL vs ARTIFACT:
  * REAL sweep  : several consecutive 1M/5M candles through the level, minute
                  volume 10-100x the ticker median, price keeps running past
                  the level. Example (TMOS@ 2026-07-24 10:26-10:40): price
                  5.23->5.38, per-minute volume 1.5M/12.9M/6.4M/12.2M/5.1M vs
                  ~15k median - a resting SELL at ~5.31-5.33 fills on real tape.
  * ARTIFACT 1 - single-candle wick: one candle pokes through the level on
                  near-median volume and the close snaps back. Example (TMOS@
                  2026-03-23 07:00): 6.83 open / 7.00 high / 6.32 low / 6.77
                  close on modest volume - a BUY at -1.5% fills on the wick and
                  can stop out before the bounce.
  * ARTIFACT 2 - open-gap day: the whole move printed at the session open
                  (open = high/low, then drift). Example (TMOS@ 2026-07-21):
                  opened 5.28 at the day's high, intraday up-pokes reached only
                  ~+0.8%, so no real intraday sweep existed above the MA.
Rule: only events that are REAL sweeps on BOTH sides count toward the pass
decision; if a side's fires are mostly ARTIFACTS, that side's bracket will fill
on noise, churn fees, and stop out - reduce that side's width or reject.

Phase D - regime sanity (live regime guard suppresses BUY during a regime break).
  7. For the deepest DOWN events, check whether they are part of a sustained
     crash (look at the D scan around those dates). SAFE's -37% dip (2025-10-30)
     is exactly such a regime-break day -> live it would NOT have bought. Such
     days inflate the DOWN count and must be discounted when judging the BUY side.

DECISION
--------
Pass when, at the chosen X, B/C/D land in SAFE's ballpark (fires/yr on both
sides, multi-candle high-volume sweeps, non-trivial run past the level).
Then add the ticker to ticker_settings.json:
  deviation_percent = X          (0.0025-0.005 = 0.25-0.5%)
  sell_band_factor / buy_band_factor from the past-the-level asymmetry measured
  allow_selling / allow_buying per your intent for that instrument
Start it at 1 lot and watch for a few live days before trusting the brackets.

CAVEATS
-------
- SAFE is ultra-quiet at the 5M scale; ordinary shares are noisier. Judge each
  ticker against its own Phase B/C evidence, using SAFE as the shape reference,
  not as an absolute volume benchmark.
- The tool ignores strategy gates: a peaked day in a downtrend may still show a
  BUY fill that live would never have been placed (regime_break), and a SELL is
  only live while flat/long. Discount those.
- Deep one-day crashes skew the DOWN table (one -37% bar); they are not "X% dips
  every year", they are one regime event. Re-run with --from/--to excluding the
  crash day to see the real dip cadence.
